# dumb.

A simple, free gym log for Android. Plan routines, log your sets as you train, and watch your
lifts and body measurements change over time. Everything stays on your phone and works without
an internet connection. There are no accounts, ads or subscriptions.

## How to use

### Get started

1. Install the APK and open **dumb.**
2. Want to look around first? Go to **More → Load sample data** to add 14 weeks of example
   workouts. Remove them later with **More → Delete all workout data**.

### Make a routine

1. On the **Train** tab, create a new routine.
2. Add exercises from the library, or make your own.
3. For each exercise, set the sets, rep range, rest time and notes. You can reorder exercises,
   link two into a superset, or add warm-up sets automatically.
4. Save it. You can have as many routines as you like.

### Train

1. Start a routine from **Train**, or start an empty workout.
2. For each set, enter the weight and reps, then tap the tick. Leave a field empty and it
   fills in with what you did last time. Beat a personal record and you'll feel a buzz and
   see a trophy.
3. The rest timer starts on its own. Use **−15**, **+15** or **Skip** to adjust it.
4. Tap a set's number to mark it as a warm-up (**W**), failure (**F**) or drop set (**D**).
   Long-press the number to delete the set.
5. Tap the arrow at the top, or press back, to minimise the workout and use the rest of the
   app. Tap the green bar above the tabs to return.
6. Tap **Finish** when you're done.

### Look back

- **History:** a calendar of your training, with a dot on each workout day. Open a workout
  to edit it, save it as a routine or delete it.
- **Progress:** weekly totals, sets per muscle group, your most-trained exercises and a
  monthly report.
- **Exercise detail:** tap an exercise name to see its records and charts.

### Track your body

1. On the **Body** tab, tap **Log** to record your weight, body fat or measurements.
2. To change the chart, press and hold the round button above **Log**, slide your thumb to a
   measurement, and let go.

### More

- **Calculators:** plate loading, one-rep max and warm-up sets.
- **Settings:** units, default rest timer, bar weight, haptics and more.
- **Export backup:** saves all your data as a file you can keep.
